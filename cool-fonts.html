Go Big. Go Bold.

Twenty condensed, serif-free typefaces at Bold (700) or Black (900) weight — tall, heavy, poster-ready. All free on Google Fonts, sorted by how much stroke contrast survives at full weight.

Honesty check: stroke contrast naturally compresses as weight goes up — that's physics, not a font flaw. At Bold/Black, strokes thicken toward each other and the thin/thick difference shrinks versus a Thin or Light cut. The Big Shoulders family (especially its Inline and Stencil styles) keeps the most visible contrast even at 900, so it leads this list. The bottom entries — Anton, Bebas Neue, Oswald, Saira Condensed — are near-monoline at this weight: rock-solid, extremely popular condensed bold sans, just honestly low on contrast.
